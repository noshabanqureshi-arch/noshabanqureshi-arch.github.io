# Square Social Studio — $20K Capacity-Aware Revenue Model

Purpose: keep the long-term **CAD $20,000/month gross revenue** target compatible with a small number of service clients and a limited owner workweek.

This is a planning model, not a revenue forecast or guarantee.

All numbers below are **gross revenue before payment-platform fees, refunds, contractors, software, taxes and other business expenses**.

## Non-negotiable design constraints
- Owner workweek target: roughly 20–25 hours.
- Done-for-You ceiling: **maximum 3 active clients**.
- Default premium-client owner delivery budget: roughly 12–15 hours/client/month.
- Revenue above the service base should come primarily from reusable digital IP and recurring products.
- Do not add client #4 merely to close a revenue gap.
- Do not launch an offer until its customer delivery/access flow is ready.

## Current value ladder
Verified/live:
- 30-Day Content Starter — $27

Package-ready/provider connection pending:
- free 30 Content Ideas

Planned:
- mini-course — $69
- Toolkit — $149
- flagship — $349
- Vault — target $99/month
- Done-for-You — from $3,000/month, max 3

The planned offers must not be counted as current revenue capacity until they are actually built/launched.

# Stage 0 — Validate the entry offer
Goal: prove the current live product can attract qualified traffic and convert at all.

Working first test already documented:
- 40 Starter sales × $27 = $1,080 gross.

Focus:
- distribution,
- landing-page clarity,
- customer access,
- real buyer questions,
- purchase data.

Do not interpret a weak first test as proof that five more products are required.

---

# Stage 1 — Establish stable service cash flow
Example planning range:

### Two clients
- 2 × $3,000 = **$6,000/month service revenue**

### Three clients
- 3 × $3,000 = **$9,000/month service revenue**

At the three-client ceiling, owner delivery budget is approximately:
- 36–45 hours/month.

The remaining owner capacity must still cover:
- Square Social's own marketing,
- products/courses/Vault production,
- admin/support,
- strategy/QA,
- buffer.

Do not use all remaining hours for extra client scope.

---

# Stage 2 — Increase revenue per client before client count
Once evidence supports pricing and scope, a future average client fee may rise without adding clients.

Example:
- 3 clients × $3,500 = **$10,500/month**

This is not an automatic price increase. It requires the market, scope, results/evidence and delivery economics to support it.

Rule:
If a client repeatedly requires more than the planned owner hours, **re-scope, re-price, delegate appropriate production or decline the scope**. Do not absorb the overage indefinitely.

Recurring revenue is the bridge toward $20K without adding service accounts.

### 25 Vault members
25 × $99 = **$2,475 MRR**

### 50 Vault members
50 × $99 = **$4,950 MRR**

# Recommended scaling order
1. Make the current $27 Starter funnel work and collect real data.
2. Connect the freebie and permission-based nurture infrastructure.
3. Add at most 2–3 premium clients with strict scope and delivery-hour tracking.
4. Finish and validate the smallest course/product layer before the flagship.
5. Build the Vault only when monthly production and member access are repeatable.
6. Increase qualified traffic and customer progression through the ladder.
7. Improve prices/conversion/retention before adding service accounts.

# Monthly CEO scorecard
Track:

## Revenue
- total gross revenue,
- service MRR,
- Vault MRR,
- product/course revenue,
- refunds,
- revenue by offer.

## Capacity
- owner hours worked,
- owner service-delivery hours,
- hours/client,
- hours to produce the monthly Vault release,
- support/admin hours,
- buffer remaining.

## Funnel
- qualified traffic,
- Starter orders,
- course sales when live,
- Vault new members/cancellations when live,
- client applications/acceptance when live,
- movement between offers.

## Unit economics
- revenue per owner hour,
- payment/platform fees,
- contractor costs when introduced,
- refund/support burden by offer.

## Customer success
- access problems,
- completion/implementation signals,
- repeat purchase,
- member use/retention,
- honest feedback/reviews after real use.

---

# Decision alarms
Pause expansion and fix the system when:
- owner hours exceed the weekly/monthly ceiling repeatedly,
- a client exceeds its scope/hour budget for two months,
- Vault support begins behaving like one-to-one consulting,
- course support burden is high because the product is unclear,
- new products are being created faster than existing products are being marketed,
- acquisition depends on constant founder outreach with no owned/evergreen traffic growth,
- gross revenue rises while owner effective hourly economics deteriorate.

# Core rule
**Scale revenue per system—not workload per customer.**
