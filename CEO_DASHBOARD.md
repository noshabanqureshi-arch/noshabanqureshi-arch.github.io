# 1. Current business model

## Target audience
Small-business owners, especially service-based and expertise-led businesses that have real knowledge/customer questions but lack a repeatable content system.

## Positioning
**Show Up Simply™**

> Turn what you already know into useful content—without making social media your full-time job.

## Customer journey
Discover → Learn → Trust → Act → Transform → Retain → Advocate

## Product method
Clarify → Plan → Create → Publish → Repeat

## Capacity rules
- Owner workweek target: 20–25 hours.
- Default planned weekday: 4 focused hours; fifth hour is buffer.
- Maximum active Done-for-You clients: 3.
- Revenue growth should come from pricing, reusable IP, courses and recurring membership before additional service accounts.

# 11. Weekly CEO review
Keep to 20–30 minutes.

Ask:
1. Gross revenue this week/month?
2. Payhip Starter orders?
3. Best qualified traffic/content angle?
4. Any access/support problems?
5. Owner hours used?
6. Any client/service capacity risk?
7. What existing asset can be reused next?
8. What is the one most important blocker to remove?
9. What should we stop doing?

Then choose no more than **three priorities** for the next week.
