## Email 2 — Teach the five content jobs
**Timing:** 1 day after delivery

**Subject:** A simpler way to decide what to post

When content feels random, it is usually because every post is being asked to do everything.

Give each post one job:

- **Teach** — answer, explain, simplify.
- **Trust** — show process, standards, proof and decisions.
- **Connect** — share useful stories, values and relatable business realities.
- **Offer** — explain what you sell and who it is for.
- **Convert** — make one next step clear.

You do not need all five every day. You need enough balance that people can learn from you, understand you, understand the offer, and know what to do next.

**CTA: Choose the four pillars you will use this month**

## Email 4 — Teach the Show Up Simply method
**Timing:** 2 days after Email 3

**Subject:** The content system in five words

The Square Social Studio method is deliberately simple:

**Clarify → Plan → Create → Publish → Repeat**

Clarify the goal, audience and voice.

Plan the customer journey, content pillars and channels.

Create useful copy and video using repeatable structures.

Publish through a workflow you can maintain.

Repeat what the evidence says is working.

The point is not to create more content. It is to stop rebuilding your process from zero every week.

## Behaviour rules
- Purchasers of the $27 Starter should stop receiving generic $27 promotion and move toward the $69 mini-course or $149 Toolkit.
- Purchasers of the $69 mini-course should be shown the $149 Toolkit primarily when they need additional reusable tools.
- Toolkit buyers should be invited to the $349 flagship only when they want guided strategy and implementation.
- Flagship buyers move to customer education, implementation reminders, product updates and advocacy/referral content rather than repeated acquisition emails.
- Do not use false scarcity, false deadlines, invented testimonials or inflated claims.
