# Square Social Studio — First $1,000 30-Day Publishing Plan

Purpose: use existing ready-to-publish material to create a realistic first distribution test for the live $27 Starter without creating more products or requiring daily content production.

## Campaign
`first-1000`

## Working revenue experiment
- Live product: 30-Day Content Starter — CAD $27
- Existing internal target: 40 sales = CAD $1,080 gross
- This is a test target, not a forecast or guarantee.

## Publishing rhythm
Use the same simple rhythm taught in the Conestoga-derived strategy:
- **4 primary pieces per week**
- Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday
- schedule in advance where possible
- no weekend owner production requirement

Total primary pieces: **16 over four weeks**.

Do not create separate ideas for every platform. Adapt the same source asset.

## Primary channel rule
Start with one primary discovery channel where the target small-business audience is actually reachable. Instagram is the default working channel for this plan because the existing assets already include Reels/carousels.

Low-effort adaptations when capacity allows:
- Reel → TikTok / YouTube Short
- carousel → LinkedIn document post where the audience fit is professional/B2B
- carousel/resource → Pinterest pin
- strong teaching idea → email later, after permission-based email is active

Do not maintain all of these simply to say the brand is everywhere.

# UTM rule
For every linkable campaign post:
- `utm_campaign=first-1000`
- `utm_medium=organic-social`
- `utm_source=<platform>`
- `utm_content=<existing content slug>`

Starter purchase-intent traffic:
`https://noshabanqureshi-arch.github.io/lp/30-day-content-starter/`

Example:
`https://noshabanqureshi-arch.github.io/lp/30-day-content-starter/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic-social&utm_campaign=first-1000&utm_content=monthly-workflow-process`

Education-first traffic should go to the most relevant resource page rather than the sales page every time.

## Measurement before website analytics is connected
Use what is genuinely available:
- platform reach/views,
- saves/shares/comments as diagnostics,
- profile visits,
- link taps/clicks available from the platform,
- Payhip paid orders,
- Payhip gross Starter revenue,
- refunds/access problems.

Do not pretend a specific post caused a purchase if cross-domain purchase attribution is not available.

## Measurement after analytics is connected
Add:
- landing-page sessions by UTM source/content,
- Starter CTA clicks,
- resource → Starter movement,
- Start Here → recommended-path clicks,
- campaign landing-page conversion proxy,
- eventual purchase attribution only when technically reliable.

## Weekly Friday review — 20 minutes max
Ask:
1. Which content angle earned qualified attention?
2. Which destination earned the most useful click behaviour?
3. Were there any Payhip orders?
4. What customer question appeared in comments/DMs?
5. What one thing should be repeated next week?
6. What one thing should be changed?

Do not rebuild the entire campaign every Friday.

## Production efficiency
Batch this campaign using existing material:
- one writing/QC block,
- one carousel/design block,
- one Reel recording/voiceover block,
- one scheduling block.

The 16 primary posts should not require 16 separate strategy sessions.

## Stop conditions
Pause and diagnose before adding products or paid traffic if:
- checkout/delivery breaks,
- product-access complaints appear,
- campaign traffic is clearly unqualified,
- the content requires unsustainable owner hours,
- claims/visuals cannot be substantiated.

## What not to do during this test
- do not launch five extra products because sales are slow,
- do not change the $27 price every week,
- do not use fake countdowns or false scarcity,
- do not buy followers/engagement,
- do not promise follower or revenue outcomes,
- do not expand to every platform simultaneously,
- do not replace the tested Payhip checkout without evidence of a problem.
