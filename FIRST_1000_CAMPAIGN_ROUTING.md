# Square Social Studio — First $1,000 Campaign Routing

Purpose: keep the first revenue experiment focused on the live $27 30-Day Content Starter and route campaign traffic to the page designed for conversion.

## Campaign
`first-1000`

## Primary revenue goal
Test whether qualified small-business traffic can convert into purchases of the live **$27 CAD 30-Day Content Starter**.

This is an experiment, not a revenue guarantee.

## Destination rule

### Educational / free-intent content
Use:
`https://noshabanqureshi-arch.github.io/free/30-content-ideas/`

Only send traffic here while the page accurately shows that final Payhip freebie delivery is still being connected.

For search/education-first content, the Resources hub may be a better destination:
`https://noshabanqureshi-arch.github.io/resources/`

### Starter purchase-intent campaign traffic
Use the focused landing page:
`https://noshabanqureshi-arch.github.io/lp/30-day-content-starter/`

This page intentionally removes the full navigation and has one primary action: buy the Starter through Payhip.

### Normal website browsing / product research
Use the full product page:
`https://noshabanqureshi-arch.github.io/products/30-day-content-starter/`

## Why the routes differ
- Resources/free content = **Discover / Learn**.
- Full product page = **Trust / product research**.
- Focused landing page = **Act / campaign conversion**.

Do not send every visitor to the same page merely for convenience.

## First campaign content routing

Free/education destination:
- idea problem Reel;
- five content jobs;
- not a full-time creator;
- one question → multiple posts;
- clear over clever;
- AI without invented expertise;
- calm system vs content sprint;
- hook → value → example → action.

Focused Starter landing page:
- monthly workflow/process;
- what is inside the Starter;
- four content pillars;
- planning-session process;
- plan next month Reel;
- do I need to post every day?;
- who the Starter is for;
- actual PDF walkthrough;
- four questions before posting;
- blank → planned workflow;
- what $27 gets you;
- direct plan-before-random-post conversion content.

## Testing rule
Do not change several variables at once.

For the first useful sample:
- keep the same live product and price;
- keep campaign naming consistent;
- vary the source/content angle;
- measure page/checkout/purchase behaviour once analytics is active;
- use Payhip order data as the source of truth for completed purchases.

## Paid-media gate
Do not buy traffic until:
- the organic content has produced useful click/purchase evidence;
- site analytics/event tracking is active;
- the landing page and Payhip checkout are QA-tested;
- a daily/total test budget and stop rule are defined.
