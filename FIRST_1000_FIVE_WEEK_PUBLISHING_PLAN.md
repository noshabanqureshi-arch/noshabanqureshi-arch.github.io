# Square Social Studio — First $1,000 Five-Week Publishing Plan

**Campaign dates:** August 24–September 27, 2026  
**Campaign:** `first-1000`

Purpose: publish and learn from the 20 completed First $1K campaign assets without creating more launch content or adding service workload.

## Revenue experiment
- Live paid offer: 30-Day Content Starter — CAD $27.
- 38 sales = CAD $1,026 gross.
- Working test target: 40 sales = CAD $1,080 gross.
- This is an experiment, not a forecast or guarantee.

- **4 core posts per week**.
- **5 weeks**.
- **20 total campaign posts**.
- Planned days: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday.
- Instagram is the primary campaign test channel.
- Cross-post only when the format transfers easily.
- If a fresh Reel is not ready, use the approved static Canva cover + full caption rather than postponing the campaign.

# Weekly measurement
Use verified data only:
- platform views/reach,
- saves/shares/comments as diagnostics,
- profile visits,
- link clicks/taps where available,
- Payhip Starter orders,
- gross Starter revenue,
- refunds/access problems,
- recurring customer questions,
- owner time.

Do not claim a specific post caused a purchase unless attribution is technically supported.

# Weekly decision rule
Change **one meaningful variable at a time** after enough data exists.

- Reach but weak clicks → inspect hook/CTA/destination clarity.
- Clicks but weak sales → inspect product fit/landing-page friction.
- Sales from one topic → reuse that topic in a new format or angle.
- Repeated real question → prioritize it over generic prompts.
- Weak signal from one post → do not redesign the funnel.

# Campaign guardrails
- Do not add product #2 to this campaign.
- Do not add client #4 to solve a revenue gap.
- Do not invent testimonials, results, urgency or scarcity.
- Do not build more launch graphics before the 20 current pieces are tested.
- Do not turn cross-posting into a second content-production workload.
