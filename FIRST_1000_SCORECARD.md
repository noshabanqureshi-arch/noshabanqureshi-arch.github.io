# Square Social Studio — First $1,000 Campaign Scorecard

Use this for the **five-week `first-1000` test, August 24–September 27, 2026**.

Do not treat platform reach as the business result. Paid orders and useful customer signals matter more.

## Campaign constants
- Live offer: 30-Day Content Starter
- Price: CAD $27
- 20 posts / 5 weeks / 4 core posts per week
- Working test target: 40 paid sales = CAD $1,080 gross
- 38 paid sales = CAD $1,026 gross
- This is a test target, not a forecast or guarantee.
- Keep product/price stable during the first useful sample unless a real customer/access problem requires a change.

## Weekly decision block

### KEEP
Which message, hook, format or source earned useful attention, clicks or paid orders?

### STOP
What consumed time without producing useful traffic, learning or revenue?

### TEST
Choose **one meaningful variable** for next week.

Examples:
- hook,
- format,
- CTA wording,
- teaching angle,
- destination page.

Do not change five variables at once.

### SCALE
Which proven idea should be reused on another platform or in another format?

## Reel-format note
Reel slots are message tests first.

If fresh Reel production is not ready:
- publish the approved static cover + full caption on Instagram,
- keep the same UTM content slug and destination,
- record the actual format used,
- test the video version later as a format variable.

Do not use the retired $850-sprint video/audio.

## Owner-time rule
The campaign must fit the existing business ceiling.

Red flags:
- campaign distribution exceeds roughly 6 owner hours in one week,
- daily reactive posting becomes necessary,
- DMs become free consulting,
- new launch content is being created while the 20 finished assets remain unused.

If a red flag appears, simplify the workflow before adding volume.

## Five-week review
At the end of the test answer:
1. Which source produced the most qualified traffic?
2. Which content angle produced the most useful clicks or purchases?
3. Did free-idea traffic later move toward the Starter?
4. Did the focused Starter landing page remove or create friction?
5. What questions appeared repeatedly before purchase?
6. Were there refunds/access issues?
7. How many owner hours did the campaign consume?
8. Which messages deserve another format/angle?
9. What should stop?
10. Is there enough evidence to increase traffic, or should message/page fit improve first?

## Interpretation rule
Do not call the campaign a success solely because one post gets high reach.

The useful outcome is a clearer answer to:
**Which message + audience + source produces qualified attention and paid demand without breaking the operating model?**
