# Square Social Studio — Show Up Simply™ Content System Curriculum

Planned price: **CAD $349**
Format: self-paced flagship program
Status: curriculum architecture documented; media, final Toolkit/customer files and protected access are not complete.

## Product promise
Help a small-business owner build a repeatable content system that exists outside their head:
- one business-linked content goal,
- defined customer and brand voice,
- repeatable pillars and channel roles,
- practical writing/video/visual workflows,
- owned audience/funnel path,
- measurement, repurposing and launch process.

## Workload boundary
The flagship is a self-paced education product, not a consulting package.

MVP does not include:
- private strategy calls,
- individualized content reviews,
- weekly office hours,
- done-for-you implementation,
- unlimited questions.

Those belong to a different offer/capacity model.

# Module 1 — CLARIFY

## Lesson 1 — Choose the business outcome
Teach:
- business goal → marketing goal → content objective → KPI,
- choose one priority offer/outcome,
- avoid vanity-only goals.

Learner produces:
- 90-day business/marketing priority,
- one content objective,
- primary KPI + supporting diagnostics.

## Lesson 2 — Build the customer brief
Teach:
- segment/target/position thinking,
- customer jobs, desired outcome, friction, objections,
- customer questions and language as source material.

Learner produces:
- one primary customer brief,
- question/objection bank.

## Lesson 3 — Define positioning + brand voice
Teach:
- promise and point of difference,
- clear vs clever language,
- voice traits and tone-by-context,
- AI-ready brand guardrails.

Learner produces:
- one-page positioning/voice brief.

---

# Module 2 — PLAN

## Lesson 4 — Map the customer journey
Teach:
- Discover → Learn → Trust → Act → Retain → Advocate,
- what content needs to do at each stage,
- one primary CTA per asset.

Learner produces:
- customer-journey map tied to current offer.

## Lesson 5 — Choose content pillars + jobs
Teach:
- pillars vs formats,
- five content jobs: Teach, Trust, Connect, Offer, Convert,
- build repeatable themes from real business knowledge.

Learner produces:
- four core pillars,
- content-job mix.

## Lesson 6 — Choose channels by role
Teach:
- owned / earned / paid,
- website/resources, email, Instagram, TikTok/Shorts, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn roles,
- do not maintain a channel merely because it exists,
- integrated campaign principle.

Learner produces:
- primary channel,
- supporting channels,
- one-sentence role for each.

---

# Module 3 — CREATE

## Lesson 7 — Write useful content
Teach:
- Hook → Value → Example → Action,
- clear calls to action,
- customer-language reuse,
- anti-hype / anti-fabrication rules.

Learner produces:
- reusable caption structure,
- several real draft captions.

## Lesson 8 — Produce short-form video simply
Teach:
- video job before format,
- talking head, voiceover, B-roll, screen recording, text-led,
- captions/sound-off usability,
- batch filming.

Learner produces:
- four repeatable video formats,
- one batch shot/script plan.

## Lesson 9 — Build a visual + AI production system
Teach:
- stable Canva layout system,
- reuse before redesign,
- AI for extraction, organization and repurposing,
- human fact/claim/example check.

Learner produces:
- template/layout set specification,
- AI workflow with guardrails.

# Module 4 — PUBLISH

## Lesson 10 — Build the production pipeline
Teach:
- Idea → Draft → Design → Review → Schedule → Publish → Repurpose,
- batch similar work,
- approval/status visibility,
- content inventory.

Learner produces:
- production board/workflow.

## Lesson 11 — Adapt one idea across channels
Teach:
- one source idea → video → carousel → resource → email,
- native format adaptation,
- UTM/source tracking where appropriate,
- avoid duplicate busywork.

Learner produces:
- one multi-format content waterfall.

## Lesson 12 — Build the owned conversion path
Teach:
- useful resource/landing page,
- permission-based email,
- free → entry product → higher support,
- landing-page focus,
- customer-success/thank-you step.

Learner produces:
- simple owned funnel map.

---

# Module 5 — REPEAT

## Lesson 13 — Measure what changes decisions
Teach:
- business outcomes first,
- platform diagnostics second,
- revenue/qualified traffic/inquiries/retention,
- do not treat likes alone as success.

Learner produces:
- monthly KPI scorecard.

## Lesson 14 — Keep / Stop / Test / Scale
Teach:
- monthly review,
- one-variable A/B tests,
- evidence threshold before declaring a tactic proven,
- content audit and repurposing.

Learner produces:
- Keep / Stop / Test / Scale decisions,
- max two next-month tests.

## Lesson 15 — Run a focused campaign or launch
Teach:
- SMART campaign goal,
- stage-based message sequence,
- real deadlines only,
- owned/earned/paid roles,
- post-launch review and retention/advocacy.

Learner produces:
- focused campaign brief,
- campaign sequence,
- post-launch review template.

- free resource articles,
- short-form videos,
- email education,
- Vault foundation library,
- product onboarding.
