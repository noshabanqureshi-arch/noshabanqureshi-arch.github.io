# Square Social Studio — Founder Weekly Operating Rhythm

Purpose: run Square Social Studio inside a sustainable owner schedule rather than letting the business expand to fill every available hour.

## Capacity rule
Default planned workday: **4 focused hours**.

Optional fifth hour: **buffer**, not automatically scheduled.

Weekly target:
- planned work: ~20 hours,
- maximum normal ceiling: ~25 hours,
- do not normalize working beyond the ceiling to compensate for poor scope, too many offers or reactive client work.

The fifth hour is for:
- genuine deadline overflow,
- customer-access/support issue,
- a time-sensitive launch task,
- occasional deeper production block.

If the fifth hour is needed every day for multiple weeks, fix the system rather than redefining the workday.

---

# CURRENT PRE-CLIENT / FIRST-REVENUE RHYTHM

## Monday — Strategy + source content
### Hour 1 — CEO / numbers
- review Payhip orders/revenue,
- review any support issues,
- review last week's content signals,
- choose maximum three priorities.

### Hours 2–3 — Core content production
- use existing `FIRST_1000_30_DAY_PUBLISHING_PLAN.md`,
- write/QC the week's four primary pieces,
- reuse existing post copy/resources before creating anything new.

### Hour 4 — Evergreen asset
- improve one existing resource,
- internal links/title/meta based on evidence,
- or turn one campaign lesson into a durable resource.

Do not create a new product on Monday simply because there is spare creative energy.

## Tuesday — Design + visual production
### Hours 1–2 — Batch graphics
- create/reuse carousel layouts,
- product walkthrough visuals,
- resource graphics,
- keep one stable visual system.

### Hour 3 — Short-video prep
- scripts,
- shot list,
- B-roll/source clips,
- captions/on-screen copy.

### Hour 4 — QA
- brand voice,
- mobile readability,
- claim accuracy,
- CTA/destination,
- UTM link.

## Wednesday — Video + scheduling
### Hours 1–2 — Batch video
- film/record several short pieces in one block,
- talking head, voiceover, B-roll or screen recording,
- no separate filming session for each post.

### Hour 3 — Edit/caption
- caption-first,
- sound-off usable,
- native dimensions,
- simple production.

### Hour 4 — Schedule/publish prep
- upload/schedule approved week's content,
- verify destinations and UTM links.

## Thursday — Funnel + business infrastructure
### Hour 1 — Funnel check
- homepage / Start Here / resource / Starter path,
- checkout/delivery support,
- fix friction before building more pages.

### Hour 2 — Current external blocker
Work on only one:
- freebie Payhip,
- domain/email,
- Mailchimp,
- Search Console/analytics,
- secure premium intake.

### Hour 3 — Customer-success / offer QA
- product instructions,
- support path,
- FAQ,
- course/Vault readiness only if current launch priorities are complete.

### Hour 4 — Sales/service readiness
Before clients:
- improve premium application/process/proposal,
- qualified outreach only when active acquisition begins.

After clients:
- reserve this hour for scheduled client strategy/approval needs.

## Friday — Measurement + reset
### Hour 1 — Weekly numbers
- Payhip orders/gross revenue,
- platform diagnostics,
- qualified clicks where available,
- access/support problems,
- owner hours.

### Hour 2 — Keep / Stop / Test / Scale
- one thing to keep,
- one thing to stop/change,
- maximum one or two tests,
- do not redesign the whole business after one week.

### Hour 3 — Admin
- financial records,
- invoices/receipts,
- file organization,
- provider/account actions,
- documentation.

### Hour 4 — Next-week setup
- choose next four content pieces,
- prepare source files,
- confirm external blocker,
- leave Monday ready to start.

# WHEN 1–3 PREMIUM CLIENTS EXIST

Client work must replace blocks; it does not get added on top of a full pre-client schedule.

Target owner delivery budget:
- ~12–15 hours/client/month.

Example with three clients:
- ~36–45 service hours/month,
- roughly 9–11 hours/week average.

At that point, reduce internal Square Social production to the highest-leverage owned work:
- one content batch,
- one evergreen/search improvement,
- one product/Vault production block,
- weekly numbers/admin.

Do not continue the pre-client acquisition workload unchanged after service capacity fills.

---

# MONTHLY SERVICE RHYTHM WHEN CLIENTS EXIST

Use the documented client cycle:
- Days 1–3: Evaluate
- Days 4–7: Plan
- Days 8–14: Produce
- Days 15–30: Approve + Publish

Batch similar work across clients where confidentiality permits operationally:
- analytics review block,
- writing block,
- design assembly block,
- scheduling-prep block.

Client data/assets remain isolated even when work types are batched.

---

# OWNER-HOUR ALARMS

Review immediately when:
- average week exceeds 25 hours for 2 consecutive weeks,
- more than one day/week becomes reactive rather than scheduled,
- customer support regularly exceeds its product/service budget,
- a premium client exceeds the agreed owner-hour budget for 2 months,
- Vault monthly production exceeds the planned 6–8 hours after the system is established,
- a course creates ongoing individualized support,
- content production requires net-new ideas every day,
- admin/provider work repeatedly consumes more than one full day/week.

Response order:
1. stop low-value work,
2. simplify scope,
3. batch/template/automate,
4. re-price where justified,
5. delegate production when economics support it,
6. decline work,
7. only then consider increasing owner hours—and not as the permanent fix.

# Core rule
**A fifth hour is a buffer. It is not a business model.**
