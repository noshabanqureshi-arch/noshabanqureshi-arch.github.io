# Square Social Studio — Measurement & Experiment Plan

This file connects business goals to website, email, product, course, social and video metrics so Square Social Studio can improve from evidence rather than vanity metrics.

## 1. Measurement hierarchy
Always measure in this order:

**Business outcome → marketing objective → funnel stage → KPI → diagnostic metric → decision.**

A metric is only useful if it helps make a decision.

## 2. Core business dashboard
Review monthly.

### Revenue
- Total revenue
- Revenue by offer
- Recurring revenue from Vault when live
- Done-for-you recurring revenue
- Average order value
- Repeat-purchase revenue
- Refund rate

### Audience / acquisition
- Qualified website sessions
- Traffic by source/channel
- New email subscribers with valid consent basis
- Cost per qualified lead when paid media begins

### Funnel
- Free-resource landing-page conversion rate
- Free resource → $27 Starter conversion
- $27 → $69 conversion
- $69 → $149 conversion
- $149 → $349 conversion
- Product/course → Vault conversion when live
- Qualified premium-service applications
- Premium-service close rate

### Retention / advocacy
- Repeat purchase rate
- Vault retention/churn when live
- Customer support issues by category
- Recommendation/NPS-style feedback
- Honest review volume
- Referral/earned traffic
- Permissioned UGC/customer examples

## 3. Funnel-stage KPIs

### Discover / Awareness
Primary outcomes:
- qualified reach,
- search impressions,
- relevant website sessions,
- video views from target audiences.

Diagnostics:
- impressions,
- reach,
- social share of voice when measurable,
- percentage of non-follower/new viewers,
- search queries.

### Learn / Consideration
Primary outcomes:
- resource consumption,
- email opt-in,
- meaningful content engagement.

Diagnostics:
- resource-page clicks,
- time/engaged sessions,
- video retention/completion,
- saves,
- email clicks,
- FAQ/product-page visits.

### Trust / Desire
Primary outcomes:
- repeat visits,
- product comparison/offer interest,
- deeper email/course interest.

Diagnostics:
- return visitors,
- pricing/product page views,
- course page visits,
- testimonial/review interactions when real proof exists,
- email reply/questions.

### Act / Conversion
Primary outcomes:
- purchase,
- membership join,
- qualified service application.

Diagnostics:
- checkout clicks,
- checkout completion,
- conversion rate,
- revenue per visitor/subscriber,
- application completion.

### Retain / Delight
Primary outcomes:
- successful use,
- repeat purchase,
- continued membership,
- lower support friction.

Diagnostics:
- product access/download completion where available,
- course completion,
- support volume,
- churn,
- follow-up engagement.

### Advocate
Primary outcomes:
- referrals,
- honest reviews,
- earned media,
- permissioned UGC.

Diagnostics:
- earned mentions,
- referral traffic,
- review response,
- recommendation/NPS-style results.

## 4. Website event map
When analytics is connected, configure events around meaningful actions rather than page views alone.

Recommended event names:
- `lead_magnet_click`
- `lead_magnet_complete`
- `starter_product_view`
- `starter_checkout_click`
- `starter_purchase`
- `course_view`
- `course_checkout_click`
- `course_purchase`
- `vault_interest_click`
- `vault_join`
- `dfy_service_view`
- `dfy_application_start`
- `dfy_application_submit`
- `email_signup`
- `resource_article_cta_click`

Do not add placeholder analytics IDs to production. Implement tracking only when the real analytics property, consent/privacy approach and testing process are ready.

## 5. Video measurement
Match video metrics to the video’s job.

### Discovery video
- first seconds retention,
- qualified views/reach,
- non-follower/new-viewer reach,
- profile/site actions.

### Teaching video
- average watch time,
- completion rate,
- saves,
- useful comments/questions,
- resource clicks.

### Conversion video
- CTA click-through rate,
- landing-page visits,
- conversion rate,
- assisted revenue when trackable.

Do not declare a video successful only because it has high views.

## 6. Email measurement
Track:
- confirmed/valid subscriber growth,
- delivery/bounce rate,
- click rate,
- unsubscribe rate,
- product-page clicks,
- conversion by email/sequence,
- revenue per subscriber,
- replies/questions,
- movement to the next value-ladder level.

Open rate may be used as a rough diagnostic where the platform reports it, but privacy features can reduce its reliability. Do not make major decisions from opens alone.

## 7. A/B testing rule
Test one meaningful variable at a time.

Possible variables:
- headline,
- CTA wording,
- hero visual,
- landing-page preview,
- email subject line,
- email CTA,
- video hook,
- video length,
- thumbnail,
- offer framing,
- send/publish timing.

Do not change headline + visual + CTA + price in one test and call the result conclusive.

## 9. Paid-media gate
Paid promotion starts only when:
- organic/product evidence supports the offer or message,
- destination page and checkout work,
- conversion measurement is ready,
- budget is capped,
- stop-loss condition is defined,
- one clear audience is targeted.

Paid media is an amplifier, not a substitute for offer validation.

## 10. Monthly review questions
1. What generated actual revenue or qualified leads?
2. Which traffic sources produced buyers, not just visits?
3. Which content moved people to the next stage?
4. Which product created the most support friction?
5. What questions did customers repeat?
6. What content should be repurposed?
7. What should be stopped?
8. What one variable should be tested next month?
9. Is growth creating more owner hours than the model allows?
10. What can be automated, templated or delegated?

## 11. Quarterly review
Every quarter evaluate:
- revenue mix between labour-dependent and scalable offers,
- customer acquisition vs repeat/advocacy contribution,
- best-performing audience segment,
- best-performing channel,
- weakest funnel handoff,
- product gaps,
- owner workload,
- whether a new channel/product is justified by evidence.

The goal is not to publish more. The goal is to make better business decisions from what is already being published and sold.
