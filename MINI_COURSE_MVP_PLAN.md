# Square Social Studio — Mini-Course MVP Plan

Course: **Show Up Simply™: Plan Your Content Month**

Subtitle: *A self-paced mini-course for building a realistic 30-day content plan in about an hour.*

Planned price: **CAD $69**

Status: five-lesson curriculum/structure exists; not for sale until media and protected learner access pass QA.

## Product rule
This course solves one problem:

> Help a small-business owner turn one real business goal and existing expertise into a realistic month of content.

Do not expand the first course into a comprehensive social-media certification.

## Naming rule
Avoid generic “30 days of content in X minutes” positioning as the primary product name. The branded title should remain tied to **Show Up Simply™** and the actual outcome: planning the month.

## Format
Target total teaching time: **45–60 minutes**.

Recommended production:
- five pre-recorded lessons,
- roughly 8–12 minutes each,
- slides/screen demonstration + voiceover is sufficient,
- captions/transcript for every lesson,
- no live calls required,
- no cohort schedule required,
- no community requirement for MVP.

The value comes from completing the planning work, not from video length.

## Lesson 1 — Clarify
Target: 8–10 minutes.

Teach:
- choose one 30-day business outcome,
- choose the offer/priority,
- choose the customer segment,
- identify customer problem, desired outcome, questions and objections.

Learner finishes:
- one goal,
- one priority offer,
- one audience brief.

## Lesson 2 — Plan
Target: 8–10 minutes.

Teach:
- content pillars,
- five content jobs: Teach, Trust, Connect, Offer, Convert,
- map useful themes to the customer journey.

Learner finishes:
- four repeatable content pillars,
- a rough content-job mix for the month.

## Lesson 3 — Build the idea bank
Target: 10–12 minutes.

Teach:
- reuse before create,
- customer questions as source material,
- notes/documents/old posts/offers as source material,
- repurpose one useful answer into multiple formats,
- AI for organizing source material without inventing facts.

Learner finishes:
- a bank of up to 30 content ideas.

Important: 30 ideas are options, not a requirement to publish 30 times.

## Lesson 4 — Turn ideas into usable content
Target: 10–12 minutes.

Teach:
- Hook → Value → Example → Action,
- one primary CTA,
- simple short-form video structure,
- how to adapt one source idea into caption/Reel/carousel/email.

Learner finishes:
- first-week drafts or outlines,
- at least one short-form video plan.

## Lesson 5 — Calendar + Repeat
Target: 8–10 minutes.

Teach:
- choose a realistic publishing rhythm,
- batch similar production tasks,
- schedule/select publish dates,
- basic business-linked measurement,
- Keep / Stop / Test / Scale review.

Learner finishes:
- realistic monthly calendar,
- first production batch,
- month-end review plan.

## Included tools
MVP should include only assets that actually improve completion:
- verified 30-Day Content Starter workbook,
- existing companion PDF planning sheets,
- reusable AI prompts,
- course quick-start/checklist,
- lesson transcripts/captions.

Do not promise an editable Canva companion pack unless that specific customer file actually exists and passes QA.

Do not add bonus files simply to increase the bundle count.

## Production reuse
The course should be built from teaching Square Social already owns:
- ten evergreen website resources,
- 30-Day Content Starter,
- existing first-$1,000 post copy,
- existing video scripts,
- Conestoga-derived frameworks already translated into Square Social operating systems.

Each lesson can later produce:
- one evergreen article/update,
- 2–4 Reels/Shorts,
- one carousel,
- one email lesson,
- Vault foundation-library content.

Create once; adapt many times.

## Learner experience
1. Purchase/access.
2. See a short Start Here page.
3. Download/open workbook.
4. Watch Lesson 1 and complete its output before Lesson 2.
5. Continue through five lessons.
6. Finish with an actual month planned.
7. Receive implementation/support emails when the permission/customer-email system is ready.

## No-live-support rule for MVP
The $69 course does not include:
- private consulting,
- content review for each learner,
- custom strategy,
- weekly calls,
- unlimited questions,
- account management.

Those services belong at a different price/capacity level.

## QA gate before sale
Do not open checkout until all are true:
1. Five lesson videos/audio are complete.
2. Captions/transcripts are complete.
3. All promised downloads exist and match the sales page.
4. Protected learner access works on mobile.
5. Purchase → access flow is tested.
6. Start Here / completion path is clear.
7. Support path is clear.
8. Refund/access terms match the actual delivery platform.
9. No personal/private files are exposed through public GitHub.

## Success metrics
After launch track:
- sales,
- purchase-to-start rate where measurable,
- lesson/course completion signals where available,
- support questions,
- refund/access issues,
- learner-reported completion of the monthly plan,
- movement to Toolkit/Vault only when it solves a genuine next problem.

Do not optimize only for course sales if buyers are not finishing the promised outcome.
