# Square Social Studio

Square Social Studio is a digital content-systems brand for small-business owners. It helps service-based and expertise-led businesses turn what they already know into clear, useful social media content without making content creation another full-time job.

**Brand promise:** Show Up Simply™

**Customer journey:** Discover → Learn → Trust → Act → Transform → Retain → Advocate

**Production method:** Clarify → Plan → Create → Publish → Repeat

## Commercial model
Square Social Studio is deliberately designed not to become a high-volume agency. Growth should come from a combination of digital products, self-paced education, recurring membership and a maximum of three premium done-for-you partnerships.

Premium-service scaling rule: improve pricing, scope, batching and production leverage before adding owner hours. The default business design keeps client count at three and grows the rest of revenue through intellectual property and recurring products.

## Payment rule
- Keep the tested $27 Starter on Payhip.
- Keep one-time digital products/courses on a protected delivery platform when that reduces owner admin.
- Use Stripe Billing for the future Vault only after the membership delivery/access system passes QA.
- Use simple Stripe recurring billing or hosted invoices for the maximum three premium clients rather than building a custom payment stack.
