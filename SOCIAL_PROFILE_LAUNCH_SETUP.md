## Profile visual rule
Use the existing Square Social brand system. Do not create a new visual identity for each platform.

Keep:
- navy / cream / gold / sage palette,
- clear serif display typography + readable body type,
- simple diagrams/checklists,
- real workbook/process visuals where useful.

Avoid:
- fake client dashboards,
- invented reviews,
- stock-photo-heavy agency imagery,
- visual trends that make the brand unrecognizable.

## CTA hierarchy
1. Unsure visitor → Start Here diagnostic.
2. Idea/search visitor → free resource.
3. Planning bottleneck → $27 Starter.
4. Established business wanting outsourcing → Done-for-You fit check, but do not make this the first-$1,000 campaign's main CTA.

## Comment/DM boundary
Do not promise 24/7 responses or free consulting in DMs.

Use short replies that:
- answer simple factual questions,
- point to the relevant free resource/product page,
- move customer-support issues to the official support route when one is configured,
- avoid diagnosing a whole business strategy inside comments.

## First profile QA
Before publishing the campaign:
- profile name clearly says Square Social Studio,
- bio matches the current positioning,
- link opens `/go/` correctly,
- three pinned pieces are accurate,
- no unfinished product is described as available,
- no personal contact data is unnecessarily public,
- the $27 price and product scope match Payhip.
