# Square Social Studio — Vault MVP Plan

Purpose: design a recurring offer that creates meaningful monthly value for small-business owners without becoming a second high-volume client-service business for the owner.

## Position
The Square Social Vault is the middle layer between a one-time download/course and Done-for-You.

It is for a small-business owner who:
- wants fresh structure and ideas each month,
- is willing to customize/publish their own content,
- does not need an agency,
- wants a growing library rather than another long course.

Target price remains a planning target of **CAD $99/month** until customer demand and the first release are validated.

## Capacity rule
The Vault must be productized.

Owner production target after the system is established:
- **one focused production block per month**, ideally 6–8 owner hours or less,
- no individualized monthly content calendars for members,
- no unlimited member requests,
- no daily community moderation requirement,
- no promise to chase every trend/platform update.

If the recurring deliverable routinely requires more than this, reduce scope or delegate production before scaling membership.

## MVP monthly release
Instead of creating 30 completely unrelated pieces every month, build a smaller set of strong source assets designed for repurposing.

Each monthly pack should include:

### 1. Monthly campaign map
One practical theme with:
- customer problem,
- business goal options,
- Discover → Learn → Trust → Act sequence,
- optional offer/CTA ideas,
- what to measure.

### 2. 12 core content prompts
Three per customer-journey job:
- 3 Discover
- 3 Learn
- 3 Trust
- 3 Act

Each prompt includes:
- purpose,
- hook direction,
- what real business information to add,
- one suggested CTA,
- repurpose suggestions.

These 12 source prompts can create many more than 12 posts when adapted into Reels, carousels, email, Stories and resource content.

### 3. Four short-form video scripts
One each for:
- FAQ/teaching,
- process/trust,
- objection/decision,
- offer/action.

Scripts must work with simple talking head, voiceover, screen recording or B-roll.

### 4. Six reusable Canva layouts
Keep layouts system-based rather than rebuilding an entirely new design pack each month:
- teaching carousel,
- checklist/process carousel,
- FAQ graphic,
- quote/point-of-view graphic,
- offer explainer,
- Reel cover.

Monthly production can refresh examples/content while the layout system remains stable.

### 5. Hook + CTA bank
A small monthly bank of adaptable openings and next-step language tied to the campaign theme.

### 6. One AI workflow
One reusable prompt/process that helps the member extract, organize or repurpose their own expertise without inventing facts, proof or customer stories.

### 7. One implementation sheet
A one-page monthly planner:
- goal,
- audience,
- selected prompts,
- publish dates,
- CTA,
- result/learning.

## Growing foundation library
The recurring value should come partly from a growing library so the monthly release does not need to become larger every month.

Foundation library can eventually include:
- brand voice quick-start,
- content-pillar builder,
- five content jobs guide,
- monthly planning workflow,
- repurposing workflow,
- basic Reel workflow,
- AI guardrails,
- measurement quick-start,
- campaign/launch checklist.

Reuse existing Square Social teaching and products wherever licensing/product boundaries allow; do not recreate identical material solely to fill the Vault.

## First three planned release themes

### Release 1 — Questions → Content
Built from existing Square Social teaching.
- find repeated customer questions,
- turn one answer into multiple formats,
- FAQ/teaching Reel scripts,
- question-led hooks,
- CTA options by funnel stage.

### Release 2 — Make the Process Visible
- behind-the-scenes without oversharing,
- standards and quality checks,
- how-it-works content,
- process Reels/carousels,
- trust-building CTA choices.

### Release 3 — Explain the Offer Without Selling Every Day
- who it is for/not for,
- what is included,
- objections,
- comparisons/use cases,
- decision content,
- direct CTA content only where appropriate.

## Member experience
A member should be able to:
1. open the current monthly pack,
2. choose one business goal,
3. select 4–8 core prompts rather than using everything,
4. customize with real expertise,
5. adapt those ideas into chosen formats,
6. publish at a realistic rhythm,
7. review results,
8. return next month without rebuilding the system.

## Community/support model
Do not launch with a high-maintenance community promise.

MVP support should be:
- clear tutorials/FAQ,
- product support for access/download issues,
- optional periodic member survey for future-pack ideas.

A live community, office hours or critique sessions should be added only if members demonstrate demand and the owner-hour budget supports it.

## Launch readiness gate
Do not activate paid subscriptions until all are true:
1. Member/delivery platform selected and tested.
2. Foundation library is usable.
3. Release 1 is fully loaded.
4. Release 2 is substantially prepared before launch.
5. Stripe sandbox subscription flow passes purchase, failed-payment, portal and cancel-at-period-end tests.
6. Recurring terms/privacy/support path are accurate.
7. Monthly production can fit inside the owner-time target.

## Success metrics
Early Vault metrics should prioritize:
- trial-to-paid only if a trial is ever introduced,
- paid member count,
- monthly churn/cancellations,
- asset/download usage where measurable,
- member-reported implementation,
- support burden per member,
- hours required to create each monthly release,
- recurring revenue per owner production hour.

Do not treat passive subscriptions from people who never use the product as the only definition of success.
