Our Edmonton story

To make showing up online feel lighter.

Square Social Studio was created in Edmonton by a Canadian founder who understands what it means to build while carrying a full life. The studio is for business owners doing a lot with a little: women building from home, local service providers serving their communities, coaches sharing their expertise, and new entrepreneurs learning as they go.

The idea began with one repeated frustration: most marketing advice adds another job to an already full day. Our products take the opposite approach. They turn broad advice into one clear next step with practical prompts, thoughtful templates, and repeatable systems—without jargon, judgment, pressure, or a hundred tabs open.

The person behind the system

Built by someone who needed simpler tools too.

The founder’s perspective combines social media marketing study with lived experience managing competing priorities. That is why every resource is written for a real week—not an imaginary schedule with endless creative time.

Square Social Studio believes small-business owners should not have to sound trendy, post constantly, or hand over their personality to a template. The goal is to make your expertise easier to organize and share while keeping the final message unmistakably yours.
