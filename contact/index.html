Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I receive my purchase?

These are digital products. After checkout, you receive instant access to download instructions and any included template links.

Can I customize the templates?

Yes. Products that include Canva or Notion templates are editable so you can add your own colours, logo, words, and business details.

Which product should I start with?

Choose a Launch Kit if you are defining or introducing your brand. Choose a Content System if your challenge is staying consistent month after month.

Are prices in Canadian dollars?

Yes. All store prices are shown in Canadian dollars.

Do I need Canva Pro or a paid Notion plan?

No. The editable resources are designed to work with free Canva and Notion accounts unless a product page clearly says otherwise.

Can I share a template with a client or friend?

Your purchase is licensed for one business. You may create finished content for that business, but the original files and template links should not be redistributed.

Can you recommend a product without a call?

Yes. Use the contact form and describe your business, your main offer, and what feels hardest about content right now. We will reply with a written recommendation.

Store policies

Clear expectations before you choose.

These assignment-store policies demonstrate the information a real digital-product customer would need before purchasing.

Digital delivery

Products are delivered electronically. No physical item is shipped, and access instructions would be provided after a completed checkout.

One-business licence

You may customize and use the finished materials for one business. Original files, prompts, and template links may not be resold or redistributed.

Digital returns

Because digital files cannot be returned, purchases would normally be final. Technical access problems would be reviewed individually.

Results disclaimer

Resources support planning and consistency but do not guarantee followers, leads, engagement, sales, or revenue.
