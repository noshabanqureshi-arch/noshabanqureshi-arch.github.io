Contact & support

Start with the fastest route to the answer.

Square Social Studio is designed to be low-touch and asynchronous. Purchases and downloads are delivered through Payhip, while a permanent branded Square Social Studio support inbox is being finalized.

Order or download problem?

Open the Payhip receipt or download email connected with the order. That record is the best starting point for accessing the file again and identifying the transaction.

If the issue is with Payhip's checkout or delivery platform itself, use Payhip's support options from the Payhip order/download experience.

Open the Starter on Payhip

Before asking for product help

  • Keep the product name.
  • Keep the email used at checkout.
  • Save a screenshot if the issue is visual.
  • Keep the Payhip receipt/order record.

Do not post order details, payment information or personal information publicly.

Product questions

The permanent Square Social Studio branded support inbox will be published here after the business domain and email setup are finalized. A personal email address is intentionally not published during launch.

Until then, the live 30-Day Content Starter has been designed to be self-serve: the Payhip download contains the workbook, companion planning pack, Start Here instructions and licence.

Quick answers

How are paid purchases delivered?

Through Payhip after checkout. The current $27 Starter delivery flow has been tested on mobile through a zero-dollar test order.

Does the $27 Starter need Canva?

No. The current Starter is a finished PDF package.

Can I share the paid files?

No. Standard purchases are licensed for one purchaser's own business use unless a product expressly grants broader rights. See the Digital Product Licence.

Are prices in Canadian dollars?

Yes, unless a checkout clearly states otherwise.
