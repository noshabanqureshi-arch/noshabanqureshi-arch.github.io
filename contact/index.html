Quick answers

How are purchases delivered?

Paid resources are digital. Checkout will only be enabled after protected delivery has been tested end to end.

Can I customize the templates?

Yes. Products that include Canva or Notion templates are intended to be adapted to your own business.

Can I share the source files?

No. Purchases are licensed for one business unless a different licence is stated.

Are prices in Canadian dollars?

Yes. Square Social Studio prices are listed in CAD.
