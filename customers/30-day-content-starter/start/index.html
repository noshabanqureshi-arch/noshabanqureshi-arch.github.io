30-Day Content Starter · quick start

Do not try to finish the whole workbook at once.

The fastest way to get value is to make the highest-leverage decisions first: the goal, customer and content pillars. The rest gets easier once those are clear.

First 10 minutes

Choose one 30-day goal.

Pick the single business outcome this month of content should support. Avoid vague goals like “more engagement.”

Next 10 minutes

Define the person you are talking to.

Write the problem, desired outcome, common objections and questions that matter most to this audience.

Next 15 minutes

Choose four repeatable pillars.

Use themes broad enough to repeat but specific enough to help you decide what belongs in the month.

Next 20 minutes

Build the idea bank.

Use the workbook prompts and your real business knowledge to create options. You do not have to publish all thirty ideas.

Then

Put only realistic ideas on the calendar.

Choose the publishing rhythm you can actually maintain. Save unused ideas for later.

End of month

Review and repeat.

Look at useful business-linked signals, identify what to keep, stop, test or scale, and carry the strongest ideas forward.

Important: AI can help organize or repurpose what you know, but you remain responsible for facts, examples, claims and the final brand voice. Do not ask AI to invent testimonials, results or expertise.
