You have the idea bank

Now choose five ideas worth using.

The free guide is meant to remove blank-page friction—not create a new obligation to publish thirty times. Pick the ideas that best match the customer, offer and business goal you care about right now.

Step 1

Choose one business goal.

What should this month of content support: awareness, qualified traffic, inquiries, a particular offer or customer education?

Step 2

Circle five useful ideas.

Prioritize the questions and topics your customers already care about. Ignore the rest for now.

Step 3

Give each idea one job.

Teach, Trust, Connect, Offer or Convert. One piece of content does not need to accomplish every goal at once.

Step 4

Decide whether you need a full month.

If the problem is no longer ideas but organizing them into a realistic monthly system, the 30-Day Content Starter is the next paid step.
