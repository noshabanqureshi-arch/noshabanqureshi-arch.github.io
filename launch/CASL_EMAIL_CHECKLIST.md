# Square Social Studio — CASL Email Launch Checklist

Use this checklist before connecting the free-resource form or sending marketing email.

## Signup form
- [ ] Clearly identify Square Social Studio / legal business operator.
- [ ] State what the subscriber is agreeing to receive.
- [ ] Provide required business contact information near the consent request.
- [ ] Use an unchecked opt-in control for optional marketing where appropriate; do not hide marketing consent inside unrelated terms.
- [ ] Record the signup source, date/time and consent language/version.
- [ ] Link the Privacy Policy.

Suggested plain-language consent line for final review:

> Send me the free guide and occasional Square Social Studio emails about practical content systems, products and courses. I can unsubscribe at any time.

Add the legally required identification/contact details before launch.

## Every commercial marketing email
- [ ] Sender identity is clear.
- [ ] Required contact information is present.
- [ ] A functioning unsubscribe mechanism is prominent and easy to use.
- [ ] The content matches the kind of communication the subscriber consented to receive.
- [ ] No deceptive subject line or sender information.

## Record keeping
Maintain records sufficient to show:
- [ ] express-consent forms and the wording presented;
- [ ] date/time/source of consent;
- [ ] implied-consent basis and expiry where relied upon;
- [ ] unsubscribe requests;
- [ ] action taken on unsubscribe requests;
- [ ] current compliance procedures and staff/automation settings.

## Transactional vs. marketing messages
Order confirmations, download/access instructions and responses to customer requests may have a different legal basis than optional promotional email. Keep optional marketing enrollment distinct from necessary transaction delivery.

## Welcome sequence — planned
1. **Delivery:** give the 30 Content Ideas resource immediately and explain how to use it.
2. **Use it:** teach the five content pillars and invite the subscriber to choose one bottleneck.
3. **Next step:** introduce the 30-Day Content Starter as the logical paid continuation.
4. **Proof/process:** show the Show Up Simply™ method and how planning reduces starting-from-zero.
5. **Offer:** invite the subscriber to choose the Starter, mini-course or Toolkit depending on the level of support needed.

## Final launch dependencies
- [ ] Select email provider.
- [ ] Configure confirmation/consent records.
- [ ] Configure sender business address/contact information.
- [ ] Configure unsubscribe footer.
- [ ] Test signup from mobile.
- [ ] Test unsubscribe.
- [ ] Confirm an unsubscribed address is suppressed from future marketing.
