**Status: internal launch draft — review before publishing.**

A purchase gives the purchaser a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the purchased materials for the purchaser’s own business or personal professional development.

- edit designated editable templates for your own brand;
- use prompts, planning systems and worksheets to create your own original business content;
- publish the final social posts, captions, Reels, emails or business materials you create using the resource;
- make reasonable backup copies for your own use;
- access course materials for your own learning.

### You may not
- resell, redistribute, sublicense, gift or publicly upload the original or substantially unchanged product files;
- share paid download links, course logins or source-template links with another person or business;
- sell the Square Social Studio templates, prompt library, workbook pages, lesson scripts or course structure as a competing digital product;
- copy substantial portions of the educational material into another course, membership, prompt library, template pack or paid resource;
- remove Square Social Studio ownership/licence notices for the purpose of redistributing the original resource;
- represent Square Social Studio educational material, templates or scripts as your own original product;
- use one purchase as a multi-client agency licence unless a separate commercial/agency licence is offered.

## Client-service use
A standard licence does not automatically authorize delivery of the editable source templates or course files to clients. A service provider may use what they learn to create original client work, but redistribution of the purchased source files requires a separate licence if Square Social Studio later offers one.

## AI use
You may use the included prompts and frameworks with AI tools for your own licensed business use. You remain responsible for reviewing outputs and for ensuring your content is accurate, lawful and consistent with your business.

Do not upload paid Square Social Studio source files to a public dataset, public prompt library, model-training repository or other system where the files become available to third parties.

The licence may be terminated for material breach, including unauthorized redistribution or resale. Termination does not affect rights/remedies that may already have arisen.

## No transfer of trademarks
Purchase does not grant any right to use Square Social Studio, Show Up Simply™ or other brand identifiers as your own brand or imply sponsorship/endorsement.

If a product needs different rights—for example, a future agency/commercial licence—the product page and checkout should clearly identify those terms before purchase.
