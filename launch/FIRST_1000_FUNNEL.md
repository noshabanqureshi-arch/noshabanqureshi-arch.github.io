# Square Social Studio — First $1,000 Funnel

## Goal
Generate the first **$1,000+ CAD in gross sales** from the $27 **30-Day Content Starter** without relying on the unfinished courses.

## Revenue math
- Price: **$27 CAD**
- Sales required to exceed $1,000: **38**
- Gross revenue at 38 sales: **$1,026 CAD**
- Working 30-day target: **40 sales = $1,080 CAD gross**

## Primary KPI
Drive **1,000 qualified visits** to the 30-Day Content Starter sales page during a 30-day test window.

Working purchase target: **4%**.
- 1,000 visits × 4% = 40 sales
- 40 × $27 = $1,080 CAD gross

This 4% target is a test assumption, not a forecast or guarantee. Current Shopify guidance for low-ticket ecommerce landing pages under $150 cites roughly 3–5% as a general conversion range. Measure Square Social Studio's actual rate and replace the assumption with real data as soon as possible.

## Funnel

### Lane A — Direct purchase
Organic social / search / referral / email
→ 30-Day Content Starter sales page
→ Stripe hosted checkout
→ secure delivery
→ post-purchase onboarding

### Lane B — Nurture
Organic social / search / referral
→ free 30 Content Ideas resource
→ email opt-in + delivery
→ welcome sequence
→ $27 Starter
→ Stripe hosted checkout
→ secure delivery

Do not require Lane B for every buyer. Warm visitors should be able to buy directly.

## Traffic target
30-day goal: **1,000 qualified product-page visits**
- About 250 per week
- About 33 per day on average

A qualified visit means the person reasonably matches the intended audience: a service- or expertise-based small-business owner who needs a simpler content planning system.

## Conversion checkpoints
Track these separately:
1. Landing-page sessions
2. Freebie opt-ins
3. Product-page sessions
4. Checkout starts
5. Completed purchases
6. Gross revenue
7. Refunds / access problems
8. Email engagement
9. Upsell / repeat purchase later

## Decision rules after first 250 product-page visits
- **Below 2% purchase rate:** do not add products. Fix traffic quality, offer clarity, proof, checkout friction or page messaging.
- **2–3%:** keep testing page copy, CTA placement and source quality.
- **3–5%:** healthy initial low-ticket range; increase qualified traffic while improving the funnel.
- **Above 5%:** preserve the winning message and test scaling carefully before changing the offer.

Do not treat likes or follower growth as the primary success metric.

## Content acquisition plan
Use the same five content jobs built into the product:
- **Teach:** solve one small content-planning problem.
- **Trust:** show the planning process and how the system works.
- **Connect:** explain the point of view behind Show Up Simply™.
- **Offer:** demonstrate what the $27 Starter includes and who it is for.
- **Convert:** invite the person to download the free guide or buy the Starter.

A separate `FIRST_1000_CONTENT_PLAN.md` contains the 20-post acquisition campaign.
