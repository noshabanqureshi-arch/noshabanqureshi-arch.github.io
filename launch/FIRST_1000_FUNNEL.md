## First review point
After the first useful sample of qualified Starter traffic:
- **Below 2% purchase rate:** check traffic quality, offer clarity, proof expectations, page messaging and checkout friction before adding products.
- **2–3%:** keep testing message and traffic source.
- **3–5%:** a useful initial low-ticket range for this experiment; increase qualified traffic carefully.
- **Above 5%:** preserve the winning message before changing the offer.

These are internal decision rules, not industry guarantees.
