If you never know what to post, you may not have an idea problem.

Start with a small idea bank, then sort the strongest ideas by purpose: Teach, Trust, Connect, Offer or Convert.

## POST 07 — Carousel — How to choose four content pillars
**Job:** Teach
**Destination:** Starter
**UTM content:** `four-content-pillars`

**Slides:**
1. Four content pillars are enough to make planning easier.
2. Pillar 1: the problem you solve.
3. Pillar 2: your process or method.
4. Pillar 3: customer questions and objections.
5. Pillar 4: your offer, use cases and next steps.
6. Add relevant values/stories across the pillars instead of creating a fifth random category.
7. Repeat the pillars. Change the angle.

**Caption:**
A content pillar should be broad enough to reuse and specific enough to help you decide whether an idea belongs.

If your pillars are “Monday motivation, tips, quotes and behind the scenes,” they may describe formats rather than business-relevant themes.

Build pillars around what the audience needs to understand.

Use AI to organize your expertise—not manufacture it.

**Script:**
A better prompt is not “write me a viral post.”

Try:
“Here is the customer question. Here is my actual answer. Organize it into a clear 30-second script. Do not invent statistics, testimonials or results. Flag anything that needs a real example from me.”
