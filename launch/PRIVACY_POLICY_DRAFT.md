## 1. Our privacy approach
We collect, use and disclose personal information only for reasonable business purposes and only to the extent reasonably necessary for those purposes. We aim to explain why information is collected, protect it appropriately, respect consent choices, and provide a process for access or correction requests.

Square Social Studio is an Alberta private-sector business and is expected to operate subject to Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) for activities within Alberta. Federal PIPEDA may apply to personal information involved in interprovincial or international commercial transactions.

## 2. Information we may collect
Depending on how you use the website or store, we may collect:
- name and contact information you provide;
- email address and consent/communication preferences;
- order information, product purchased, amount, currency and transaction status;
- account/course progress information when a course platform is used;
- customer support messages and information you choose to provide;
- technical information such as IP address, browser/device type, pages visited and referral source when analytics/cookie tools are enabled;
- survey, quiz or feedback responses you choose to submit.

Payment card information should be processed by the selected payment processor/platform and should not be stored in the public Square Social Studio website repository.

## 3. Why we collect and use information
We may use personal information to:
- provide a requested free resource;
- process and fulfil an order;
- provide customer accounts, course access or digital downloads;
- answer customer questions and provide support;
- keep records of transactions and consent;
- send commercial email when we have a lawful basis/consent to do so;
- understand website, product and course performance;
- prevent fraud, abuse or unauthorized access;
- meet legal, tax, accounting or regulatory obligations.

We will not use personal information for a new materially different purpose without appropriate authority or consent.

## 4. Email and marketing consent
Marketing emails will be sent only when permitted by applicable Canadian anti-spam requirements. Where express consent is requested, the signup should clearly identify Square Social Studio, explain what messages the person is agreeing to receive, and provide required contact information. Marketing messages should include sender identification and a functioning unsubscribe mechanism.

We will maintain reasonable records of consent and unsubscribe requests.

Transactional messages necessary to deliver a purchase, account, download, support response or other requested service may be sent separately from optional marketing communications as permitted by law.

## 6. Cookies and analytics
Before analytics or advertising tools are activated, describe the actual cookies/technologies in use and provide any consent controls required by applicable law or the selected tools.

The initial launch should avoid unnecessary trackers. Collect only analytics that help operate and improve the business.

## 7. Retention and security
We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described, business/legal record requirements, dispute handling and legitimate security needs. When information is no longer required, it should be securely destroyed or anonymized as appropriate.

Paid files and customer information must not be stored in the public GitHub repository.

## 8. Access and correction
Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access to personal information held about them and request correction of inaccurate information. Requests should be sent in writing to [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We may need to verify identity before responding.

## 9. Withdrawal of consent
Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw or modify consent subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawing optional marketing consent will not prevent delivery of a product or service already purchased where communication is required for that transaction.

## 10. Privacy incidents
If a privacy incident occurs, we will investigate, contain and document it and take notification steps required by applicable privacy law, including notifying the appropriate privacy regulator where a breach meets the legal notification threshold.

## 11. Children
Square Social Studio products and business content are intended for business owners and adult purchasers, not for children. Do not intentionally collect personal information from children through marketing forms.

## 12. Changes
We may update this policy when the business, vendors or legal requirements change. The effective date should be updated when a revised policy is published.

## Legal reference notes for final review
This draft was structured around Alberta PIPA principles: consent/authority, reasonable purposes, limiting collection, access/correction, safeguards, retention/destruction, breach notification where required, and designation of a privacy-responsible individual. PIPEDA may apply to interprovincial/international commercial personal-information flows. CASL requirements also affect email marketing consent, sender identification, unsubscribe and consent records.
