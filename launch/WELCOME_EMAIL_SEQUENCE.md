## Email 2 — Teach the five jobs of content
**Timing:** about 1 day later

**Subject:** Your content does not all have the same job

One reason content planning gets messy is that every post is expected to do everything.

Instead, give different posts different jobs:

**Teach** — answer questions and make your expertise useful.

**Trust** — show process, standards and how you actually work.

**Connect** — share relevant values, stories and points of view.

**Offer** — explain what you sell and who it helps.

**Convert** — make one next step clear.

Take the ideas you saved yesterday and label each one with a job.

If all five are Teach posts, that is useful information. Your audience may be learning from you without ever understanding what you sell.

**CTA:** Label your five ideas Teach, Trust, Connect, Offer or Convert.

— Square Social Studio
