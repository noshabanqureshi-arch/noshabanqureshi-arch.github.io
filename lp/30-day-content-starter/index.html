Questions before buying

Clear product. Clear scope.

Do I have to post 30 times?

No. The 30 ideas form an option bank. Choose a publishing rhythm you can maintain and save unused ideas for later.

Do I need Canva?

No. This edition is delivered as finished PDF planning files. You can use your preferred design tool later when creating the actual posts.

Will this write every post for me?

No. It gives you the planning structure, formulas and prompts. Your real expertise, examples, facts and final judgment still come from the business.

Does it guarantee more followers or sales?

No. It is a planning system, not a performance guarantee. Results depend on your offer, audience, distribution, market and execution.

Can I use AI with the workbook?

Yes. The prompts are designed to help organize and adapt your real business knowledge. You remain responsible for checking accuracy and removing anything AI invents.
