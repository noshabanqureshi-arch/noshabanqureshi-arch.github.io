The curriculum

5 modules. 15 implementation lessons.

01 · Clarify

Goal · audience · voice

Choose the business outcome, build an evidence-led audience brief and document a repeatable brand voice.

02 · Plan

Funnel · pillars · channels

Map content to Discover, Learn, Trust and Act, then choose pillars and channels the business can actually support.

03 · Create

Copy · short video · visuals

Use repeatable copy structures, practical short-video production and a controlled visual system.

04 · Publish

Workflow · native formats · email

Move content through one production pipeline, adapt it for selected channels and build an owned audience.

05 · Repeat

KPIs · repurpose · launch

Measure signals that change decisions, repurpose proven ideas and run focused launches.

The outcome

The system exists outside your head.

  • One clear content/business goal and KPI framework.
  • Audience brief built around jobs, friction, objections and language.
  • One-page brand voice guide with AI-ready rules.
  • 30-day content plan mapped by content job and business need.
  • Priority-channel roles and waterfall repurposing workflow.
  • Repeatable caption and short-video production systems.
  • Consistent visual-template system.
  • Production/calendar/performance dashboard.
  • Free-resource → permission-based email → paid-offer funnel plan.
  • Monthly KPI review and repurposing process.
  • Focused launch system with honest urgency and post-launch review.
