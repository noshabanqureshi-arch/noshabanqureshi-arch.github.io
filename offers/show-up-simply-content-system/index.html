5 modules. 15 implementation lessons.

Goal · customer · voice

Business-linked objective, customer brief, positioning and AI-ready brand voice.

02 · Plan

Journey · pillars · channels

Discover → Learn → Trust → Act, repeatable content pillars and role-based channel selection.

03 · Create

Copy · video · visuals

Production workflow, one-idea/many-formats repurposing and resource/email conversion paths.

KPIs · testing · launches

The intended outcome

The system exists outside your head.

  • One clear content/business goal and KPI framework.
  • Primary customer brief built around jobs, friction, objections and language.
  • One-page positioning and brand voice guide.
  • Customer-journey map and repeatable content pillars.

  • Repeatable caption, short-video and visual production systems.
  • Content production/calendar workflow.
  • Free-resource → permission-based email → paid-offer funnel map.
