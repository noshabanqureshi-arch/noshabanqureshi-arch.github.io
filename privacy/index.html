Privacy Notice

Effective August 23, 2026

This notice explains the information flows currently used by Square Social Studio, a digital education brand operating from Alberta, Canada. It covers this website and the connected checkout and digital-delivery services used for Square Social Studio products.

What this website collects

The public Square Social Studio website is a static GitHub Pages site. Square Social Studio has not intentionally added advertising pixels or a third-party marketing analytics script to the site at this stage. The website itself does not ask for or store payment-card details.

Orders and free downloads

Digital checkout and delivery are handled through Payhip. When you request a free resource or purchase a product through Payhip, Payhip may collect information needed to create the order or download record, such as your email address, order details, currency, transaction status and technical information used to operate or secure its service.

Card payments available through Payhip are processed by connected payment providers such as Stripe. Square Social Studio does not receive or store your full payment-card number on this website.

How information is used

Information connected with an order or requested download may be used to deliver the resource, provide receipts or access links, troubleshoot delivery, keep transaction records, prevent abuse or fraud, and meet applicable business, accounting or legal obligations.

Marketing email

Requesting a product or free download is not, by itself, described on this website as consent to an ongoing Square Social Studio marketing sequence. Optional marketing email will be activated separately only when the business has the required sender identity, contact information, consent records and unsubscribe process in place.

Service providers

Current launch services include GitHub Pages for the public website, Payhip for checkout and digital delivery, and Stripe for connected card-payment processing. Those providers operate under their own privacy terms and may process information in locations outside Alberta or Canada.

Cookies and technical data

Square Social Studio has not intentionally added an advertising cookie or tracking pixel to this website. Hosting, checkout and payment providers may use cookies, logs or similar technologies necessary for their own security, functionality, fraud prevention and service operation.

Retention and security

Square Social Studio aims to keep customer and transaction information only for legitimate business, delivery, support, security and record-keeping purposes. Paid customer files and private customer information are not intentionally published in the public website repository.

Your choices and questions

If you need help with a Payhip order or download, start with the receipt or download email associated with that order. For Square Social Studio privacy or product questions, use the Contact page. A permanent branded privacy/support inbox will replace the temporary launch contact arrangement once the business domain is finalized.

Changes

This notice may be updated as Square Social Studio adds a custom domain, branded email, an email-marketing provider or analytics tools. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

Important: This is a plain-language customer notice about the current launch setup. It does not remove any non-waivable privacy or consumer rights that may apply.
