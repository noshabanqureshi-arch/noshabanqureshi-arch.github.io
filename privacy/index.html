Privacy Notice

Effective August 23, 2026

This notice explains the information flows currently used by Square Social Studio, a digital education brand operating from Alberta, Canada. It covers this website and the connected checkout, digital-delivery and temporary email-support services used during launch.

What this website collects

The public Square Social Studio website is a static GitHub Pages site. Square Social Studio has not intentionally added advertising pixels or a third-party marketing analytics script to the site at this stage. The website itself does not ask for or store payment-card details.

Orders and free downloads

Digital checkout and delivery are handled through Payhip. When you request a free resource or purchase a product through Payhip, Payhip may collect information needed to create the order or download record, such as your email address, order details, currency, transaction status and technical information used to operate or secure its service.

Card payments available through Payhip are processed by connected payment providers such as Stripe. Square Social Studio does not receive or store your full payment-card number on this website.

Email support and inquiries

During launch, Square Social Studio uses squaresocialstudio@gmail.com as a temporary business support and inquiry inbox. If you choose to email that address, Square Social Studio may receive the information you include in the message, such as your email address, name or signature, product/order context, question and any attachments you intentionally send.

Support email may be used to answer the inquiry, identify an order or access problem, troubleshoot delivery, maintain reasonable support/business records and follow up on the specific request. Do not email passwords, full payment-card details, customer lists or other sensitive information that is not necessary to resolve the issue.

Email is processed through the relevant email service providers used by the sender and Square Social Studio. A permanent branded-domain support address is planned once the business domain/email setup is finalized.

How information is used

Information connected with an order, requested download or support inquiry may be used to deliver the resource, provide receipts or access links, answer questions, troubleshoot delivery, keep transaction/support records, prevent abuse or fraud, and meet applicable business, accounting or legal obligations.

Marketing email

Requesting a product, free download or customer-support reply is not, by itself, described on this website as consent to an ongoing Square Social Studio marketing sequence. Optional marketing email will be activated separately only when the business has the required sender identity, contact information, consent records and unsubscribe process in place.

Service providers

Current launch services include GitHub Pages for the public website, Payhip for checkout and digital delivery, Stripe for connected card-payment processing, and email service providers for the temporary support/inquiry inbox. Those providers operate under their own privacy terms and may process information in locations outside Alberta or Canada.

Cookies and technical data

Square Social Studio has not intentionally added an advertising cookie or tracking pixel to this website. Hosting, checkout, payment and other service providers may use cookies, logs or similar technologies necessary for their own security, functionality, fraud prevention and service operation.

Private browser-only tools

Some Square Social Studio tools, including the Start Here diagnostic and private operator checklists, use your browser's local storage for simple on-device state such as selections or completion checks. The current versions are designed so that information remains in that browser rather than being submitted to a Square Social Studio server. Clearing browser/site data may remove that locally stored state.

Retention and security

Square Social Studio aims to keep customer, transaction and support information only for legitimate business, delivery, support, security and record-keeping purposes. Paid customer files, credentials and private customer information are not intentionally published in the public website repository.

Your choices and questions

If you need help with a Payhip order or download, start with the receipt or download email associated with that order. For Square Social Studio product or privacy questions, use the Contact page or the temporary launch support inbox listed there. A permanent branded privacy/support inbox is planned after the business domain is finalized.

Changes

This notice may be updated as Square Social Studio adds a custom domain, branded email, an email-marketing provider, analytics tools or additional customer-access systems. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

Important: This is a plain-language customer notice about the current launch setup. It does not remove any non-waivable privacy or consumer rights that may apply.
