Application questions

What the secure intake will ask.

You can prepare these answers now. Do not enter confidential customer information, passwords, financial account details or private client data.

What does your business sell, and who is the primary customer?
What is the single most important business outcome you want content to support in the next 90 days?
Which offer or service should receive the most attention?
What questions do customers repeatedly ask before buying or booking?
What existing materials can you provide: FAQs, notes, past posts, website copy, presentations, documents or video?
