A calendar-driven service

The month follows a rhythm—not your inbox.

The workflow is intentionally batched so approvals, production and reporting happen in predictable windows instead of creating daily reactive work for either side.

Questions before applying

Know what this service is—and is not.

Do we need weekly meetings?

No. The operating model is designed around structured intake, shared materials and scheduled asynchronous approvals. A call can be used when it genuinely solves a problem, but recurring meetings are not the default way work gets done.

Do you guarantee followers, leads or sales?

No. Square Social Studio can improve the strategy, clarity, consistency and execution of content, but no ethical marketing service can guarantee platform reach, follower growth, inquiries or revenue.

What does the client need to provide?

Accurate information about the business, current offers, brand materials, customer questions, approved facts/claims, existing content worth reusing, timely approvals and the agreed analytics/access needed for the contracted scope.

Will you take over passwords?

No shared-password-by-email workflow is part of the service. Delegated access and two-factor authentication should be used where the platform supports them.

Who is probably not a fit?

A business that needs daily real-time community management, 24/7 crisis monitoring, unlimited revisions, constant trend chasing, or content built around claims the business cannot substantiate is not a good fit for this structured monthly system.

Why only three clients?

The limit is intentional. The service is designed around a fixed owner-capacity budget so strategy and quality do not deteriorate as client count grows. Square Social Studio scales primarily through digital products, education and recurring intellectual property rather than adding service accounts indefinitely.
