The planned MVP

Strong source assets designed to be reused.

Instead of promising thirty completely unrelated posts every month, the Vault is planned around a smaller set of strong source ideas that members can adapt into Reels, carousels, email, Stories and other formats.

1 campaign map

One monthly theme with business-goal options and a Discover → Learn → Trust → Act sequence.

12 core prompts

Three prompts for each customer-journey job, with purpose, hook direction, real information to add, CTA and repurpose options.

4 Reel scripts

FAQ/teaching, process/trust, objection/decision and offer/action scripts that work with simple formats.

6 reusable layouts

A stable Canva layout system for teaching, checklists/process, FAQ, point of view, offer explanation and Reel covers.

Hooks + CTA bank

Adaptable openings and next-step language tied to the month’s campaign theme.

1 AI workflow + planner

A focused AI process for extracting or repurposing real expertise, plus one simple implementation sheet.

Design principle: the membership should reduce blank-page decisions, not create another giant monthly checklist. Members choose the ideas that fit their business rather than trying to publish everything.

First three planned themes

Build the recurring library from teaching Square Social already owns.

Release 01

Questions → Content

Find repeated customer questions, turn one answer into several formats, and use question-led hooks without inventing new subject matter every day.

Release 02

Make the Process Visible

Turn standards, decisions, quality checks and behind-the-scenes process into useful trust-building content.

Release 03

Explain the Offer

Who it is for, what is included, objections, comparisons, use cases and direct calls to action—without making every post a sales pitch.

Built around four customer jobs

Discover. Learn. Trust. Act.

Discover

Be found for the right problem.

Use questions, hooks and useful entry points that help the right customer recognize the need.

Learn

Teach from real expertise.

Turn what you already know into clear tips, explanations, examples and mini-guides.

Trust

Show how you think.

Use consistent voice, process and point of view to make the business easier to understand.

Act

Connect content to business.

Use one clear next step—download, buy, inquire, book or learn more—when the timing makes sense.

Built for the DIY middle

More ongoing support than a download. Less commitment than done-for-you.
